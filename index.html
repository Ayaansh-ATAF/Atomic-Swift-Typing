ATOMIC PSD · PRIMARY SECURITY DRILL

ChatterChap chat, with safer classroom conversations.

Atomic PSD applies server-side checks to classroom chat, slows message bursts, flags repeated or unauthorized activity, and gives trusted admins a review queue. ChatterChap classroom chat stays available to approved class members.

Security signals can be wrong. Reports are reviewed by an admin; this does not automatically ban accounts or guarantee protection from every attack.
